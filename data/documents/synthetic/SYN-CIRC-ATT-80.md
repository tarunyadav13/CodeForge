## Section 1. Revised Threshold

1.1 With effect from 2026-07-01 the minimum attendance required to appear in the end-semester examination of a course is 80% of the classes held, replacing the 75% stated in Section 4.1 of SYN-REG-ATT-75.

1.2 All other provisions of SYN-REG-ATT-75, including Section 7 (placement eligibility), continue to apply.
