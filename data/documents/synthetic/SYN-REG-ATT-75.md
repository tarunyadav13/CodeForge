## Section 4. Attendance for the End-Semester Examination

4.1 A student must have attended at least 75% of the classes held in a course to be eligible to appear in the end-semester examination of that course.

4.2 Attendance percentage is computed as classes attended divided by classes held, multiplied by 100. A student whose attendance is exactly 75% meets the requirement.

## Section 7. Campus Placement Eligibility

7.1 A student may register for campus placements only if the student has no active backlogs and a CGPA of at least 6.0.
